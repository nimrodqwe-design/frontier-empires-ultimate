Your first campaign

A kingdom starts with one good decision.

Welcome, ruler. Your capital is yours; the frontier beyond it is not. Use this quick-start guide to get moving.

  1. Scout the map. Select a neighbouring territory to inspect its owner, defences and rewards.
  2. Prepare your army. Recruit troops or trade for food if supplies are running low.
  3. Choose your move. March on a neighbouring territory, or strengthen your economy before risking a fight.
  4. End the day. Collect income, pay upkeep and watch rival lords make their moves.

Tip: your campaign autosaves in this browser. Use Export backup to keep a copy.
