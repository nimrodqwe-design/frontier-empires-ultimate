How battles work

Battle strength is calculated first. Then choose a limited order each round:
  • Charge: high damage, greater losses.
  • Volley: rangers deal bonus damage with reduced retaliation.
  • Guard: reduce incoming damage and hold the line.
Rival wars resolve automatically for now and appear in the chronicle.
